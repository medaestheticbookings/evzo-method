    Our rules

    Built on rules, not reviews.

    EVZO won't put words in anyone's mouth. Instead of testimonials, here are the rules it runs on.

    01

    No safe-to-go verdict. Ever.

    Readiness helps you plan. EVZO never tells anyone a trail is safe for them today — that judgement belongs to you and your guide.

    02

    Self-reported stays self-reported.

    A score built from your own answers can never pass 70, and your passport shows what you added yourself as your own statement.

    03

    No number it cannot measure.

    No member counts, no ratings, no success rates. If a figure appears, something counted it, and the page says what.

    04

    Guides are checked, not rated.

    EVZO reads a guide's licence, insurance and first-aid certificate and dates the check. No stars, no reviews, no ranking.

    05

    Only recorded walks count.

    Leaderboards count trails finished on an activity recorded in EVZO. Nothing typed in by hand, from anyone.

    06

    One figure, everywhere.

    Where published figures disagree, EVZO records one and every screen uses it. Photographs are credited to the people who took them.
