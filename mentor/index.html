Offer Builder

Framework · $100M OffersThe value equation — a buyer's perceived value rises with the dream outcome and their perceived likelihood of achieving it, and falls with the time delay and the effort and sacrifice required of them. So an offer is improved on four levers, not by lowering price.

Score the offer you have now

Answer as the clinic owner would, not as you would. Each lever 1–10.

Dream outcome — how badly do they want it?7
"A full appointment book" scores high. "Better social media" scores low, because it is not an outcome.
Perceived likelihood — do they believe you can do it?4
Zero case studies, no clinic logos, a personal Instagram. This is your weakest lever and the honest number is low.
Speed — how fast do they see the first result? (10 = fastest)6
Your 14-day "first booking or first month free" is genuinely good here. Say it earlier.
Ease — how little work is it for them? (10 = easiest)5
Anything needing their photos, their captions, their approval loop or their logins scores low.

Promises you cannot support — read before you quote anything

1 · "We'll run your Meta/Google ads" is not deliverable for a dentist or any doctor-run clinic. Checked 10 Nov 2026: Π.Δ. 39/2009 arts. 21–22 bans «κάθε άμεση ή έμμεση ενέργεια διαφήμισης ή δημοσιότητας» by dentists and expressly covers the internet; Π.Δ. 84/2001 art. 15 bans advertising by private ΠΦΥ providers «με οποιοδήποτε τρόπο», including prices; Ν. 3418/2005 art. 17 does the same for doctors and §5 extends the sanctions to ads run by third parties with the doctor's knowledge — an agency does not shield the client. ΙΣΘ states the fine as €14.000 rising to €42.000 with loss of the operating licence on repeat, and the Ministry announced AI-based sweeps of private practices in Sept 2026. Selling paid ads to that segment sells them a fine.

2 · No before/after photos, patient testimonials, prices, offers, πακέτα or «ο καλύτερος» for a ΠΦΥ client. Art. 17 §6 covers thank-you letters and success stories; the ΟΣ Πειραιά circular names offers, discounts, packages, contests, comparisons and «προβολή περιστατικών» explicitly. Half the med-spa creative playbook is unusable here.

3 · A non-medical κέντρο αισθητικής is a different animal — no medical code applies, so ads, prices, offers and consented before/afters are all fine. Ask which licence they hold on the first call. It changes the product you can sell them.

4 · Don't promise a number you've never produced. You have no clinic result yet. "8–12 appointments" is a forecast, not a promise — sell the process plus the guarantee until one clinic's real numbers exist. And €1 patient leads is not reachable; your own data says €3–4.

5 · Before/after is personal data even cropped. Written, specific, pre-publication consent or no photo — a Lithuanian clinic was fined €6.000 and its surgeon €840 where hair and a tattoo identified the patient and consent was only "almost" given.

Not legal advice. The full brief with sources is in clinic-clients/compliance-greece.md. There is case law reading the dental code as not an absolute ban — the Σύλλογοι enforce it as one, so do not build a business on that.

So the offer that is actually legal and sellable

For dentists and doctor-run med spas (ΠΦΥ): Google Business Profile and review growth · a compliant site that converts · booking, recall and no-show recovery to their existing patient list · educational content with no case photos and no prices. All of it organic or relationship-based, none of it advertising. That is a narrower product than you planned and a far easier sale: nobody else in Greece is pitching compliance as the feature.

For non-medical aesthetic centres: the full paid-ads playbook you already own. Price it higher — it costs you ad management — and keep the two segments' numbers apart, as the scope requires.

Workspace 2

Client Acquisition

Framework · $100M LeadsThere are four ways to get anyone's attention — warm outreach, cold outreach, posting free content, paid ads — and each can be done by you or by someone else on your behalf. Volume inside one of them beats dabbling in all four.

Two funnels. Never one number.

Funnel A — clinics buying from you. A €450–1.500/month client. Worth spending €100–300 to acquire.
Funnel B — patients buying from your clinics. €3–4 a lead, delivered as the service.
You already made this mistake once in Cyprus, comparing gardener-recruitment CPL with homeowner CPL and chasing a number that didn't exist. Never put A and B in the same report.

Which channel, given you are one person

Cold outreach · IG DM then phone58 named clinics, owner answers the phone, costs €0, your documented order is DM → phone → email. Feedback within hours.Primary — all of it
Warm outreachThin: no clinic network yet. But every supplier, rep and dentist you already know is one introduction.Secondary — 10 asks
Free contentYou can produce it fast, but it compounds over months and you need cash in weeks. The Aesthetics Pipeline page is the proof asset, not the channel.Later — 1 post/week max
Paid ads to clinic ownersAlready running at tiny spend. At €20 payment thresholds you cannot buy enough data to learn anything, and B2B clinic-owner targeting in Greece is a small pool.Not yet — stop fiddling

Decision: one channel — cold outreach — at a volume you have never attempted. Ten DMs a day for fourteen days = 140 touches. That is the whole plan, and it is enough to produce a signed client or a clear reason why not.

IG DM · first touch · dentist

Καλησπέρα! Είμαι ο Μάρκο, δουλεύω με ιδιωτικά οδοντιατρεία στην Αθήνα.

Κοίταξα το προφίλ σας — έχετε πολύ καλές κριτικές, αλλά το Google προφίλ σας εμφανίζεται κάτω από 3–4 οδοντιατρεία της περιοχής όταν κάποιος ψάχνει «οδοντίατρος [περιοχή]». Αυτό είναι χαμένα πρώτα ραντεβού κάθε μήνα.

Σας έφτιαξα μια σελίδα με τα 3 σημεία που το προκαλούν. Θέλετε να σας τη στείλω; Χωρίς κόστος και χωρίς δέσμευση — ούτε θα σας πουλήσω διαφημίσεις, ξέρω ότι δεν επιτρέπονται στα ιατρεία.

Why it works: specific observation about their listing (not a template), the gift precedes the ask, and the last line proves you know their rules — which is the single fastest way to separate yourself from everyone else who DMs them.

IG DM · first touch · med spa / κέντρο αισθητικής

Καλησπέρα! Ο Μάρκο εδώ — δουλεύω με κέντρα αισθητικής και ιατρικής αισθητικής στην Ελλάδα.

Είδα ότι τρέχετε laser αποτρίχωση. Έκανα έναν γρήγορο έλεγχο: στη σελίδα σας δεν υπάρχει pixel, άρα ό,τι ξοδεύετε σε διαφήμιση δεν μετριέται, και τα μηνύματα στο Direct δεν καταλήγουν σε ραντεβού πουθενά καταγεγραμμένα.

Έχω ετοιμάσει ένα 1-σελιδο με το τι χάνεται και πού. Να σας το στείλω; Δωρεάν, 2 λεπτά διάβασμα.

Check the pixel before you send it, so the claim is true for that clinic. If they do have one, lead with whatever is actually missing — the specificity is the whole message.

Phone · the opener and the first 30 seconds

Opener: «Καλησπέρα, ο Μάρκο. Σας έστειλα ένα μήνυμα στο Instagram για το Google προφίλ του ιατρείου — σας βρίσκω σε κακή στιγμή;»

If "ναι, είμαι με ασθενή": «Φυσικά. Δύο λεπτά αύριο στις 2 ή στις 6;» — then hang up. Get the appointment, not the pitch.

Label the objection before they raise it: «Φαντάζομαι σας χτυπάνε συνέχεια για marketing, και μάλλον κάποιος σας έχει κάψει ήδη.»

Then ask, don't tell: «Πόσα πρώτα ραντεβού κλείσατε τον περασμένο μήνα και από πού ήρθαν;» · «Ποιος τα διαχειρίζεται τώρα;» · «Τι πληρώσατε για marketing τους τελευταίους 6 μήνες και τι έγινε;» · «Πόσα ραντεβού ακυρώνονται ή δεν εμφανίζονται;»

Close to the audit, never to the sale: «Να σας στείλω τον έλεγχο και να τον δούμε 10 λεπτά την Τρίτη;»

$100M Leads on the ask, and the questions follow The Mom Test: past behaviour and real numbers only. Never «θα σας ενδιέφερε…» — everyone says yes to that and nobody pays.

Follow-ups · the sequence that actually converts

Most of your signings will come from touch 3–5, which is exactly where you will want to stop. Log each one in the pipeline below.

  • Day 0 — DM.
  • Day 2 — the audit, sent whether or not they replied: «Σας το στέλνω όπως είπα, χωρίς δέσμευση.»
  • Day 4 — phone call.
  • Day 7 — one specific thing you noticed since: a competitor's new listing, a review they haven't answered.
  • Day 12 — the close-the-loop message: «Να το κλείσω το θέμα ή να σας ξαναενοχλήσω τον άλλο μήνα;» — this one gets the most replies of the five.

Five touches × 20 clinics = 100 actions. That is two weeks of workspace 2 and it is the entire job right now.

Money Model

Framework · $100M Money ModelsStructure the offer so the cash a new client brings in quickly covers what it cost to get them — then acquisition funds itself and growth stops being capped by your savings. The working test: gross profit collected in the first 30 days ≥ 2 × CAC.

Why this matters more for you than for most

You are funding ads €20 at a time off a personal card and the payment threshold keeps tripping. That is not a budgeting quirk — it is the symptom of a money model where cash arrives later than it leaves. Fix the structure and the €20 problem disappears.

Recommendation, with its assumptions on the table

Sell €450 setup + €450/month, 3-month minimum, first month guaranteed. Not the €900 Growth tier, and not €1.500, until one clinic has produced a number you can show.

Why: it collects €450 on day one against a CAC of roughly €120, which clears the 2× rule immediately, so client two is paid for by client one and you stop funding ads from your own pocket. It also halves the price objection at the exact moment you have zero proof — your weakest lever in workspace 1.

Assumptions — each one is falsifiable:

  • A clinic will pay a setup fee to someone with no case study. Untested.
  • Delivery is ~10 hours in month one, ~6 after. Guess — time it on client one.
  • Clients stay ~4 months. Unknown; you have never had one.
  • CAC ≈ €120 of your time at €25/h, cash CAC ≈ €0 on cold outreach.
  • You can hold ~5 clients solo before delivery quality drops.

The validation test — small, cheap, two weeks: quote exactly this structure to the first 10 clinics that take a call. Don't negotiate it, don't discount it, don't offer the other tiers. Metric: ≥2 of 10 say yes at €450+€450. Review: .
Then adjust: 0 of 10 → the setup fee is wrong for a proof-less seller, drop it to €150 and raise the retainer. 1 of 10 → the price is fine, the proof is the blocker, so deliver one clinic at cost to manufacture a case study. 4+ of 10 → you underpriced; take the next ones to €900 and leave the early clients where they are.
