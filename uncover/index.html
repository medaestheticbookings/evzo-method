Your dataWhat is kept, and where

Uncover keeps the map you have uncovered, your distance, level and settings. With an account they are stored against your email so the map follows you between phones; without one they never leave this device. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising, and there is no tracking of any kind. Delete your account and the lot goes with it.
