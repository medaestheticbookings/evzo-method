# EVZO — Meta campaign build sheet

Every field, in the order Ads Manager asks for it. Creative is in
`build/TO-ADVERTISE/`. Nothing here needs a decision made on the spot.

---

## Read this before you build it

**You asked for five ad sets, one per ad. Don't.** An ad set needs roughly 50
conversions a week to leave the learning phase and stop guessing. Five ad sets
split your budget five ways, so at anything under about €50 a day none of them
ever learn, and Meta spends the whole budget still experimenting. The five ads
are not five audiences, they are five ways of saying the same thing to the same
person.

| Daily budget | Build |
|---|---|
| Under €50 | **One ad set, all five ads inside it.** Meta finds the winner for you |
| €50–100 | Two ad sets, split by hook: M+N (dieting) vs O+P+Q (daily life) |
| Over €100 | Then, and only then, one ad set per hook |

The rest of this sheet assumes **one ad set**. Scale to the table above once
something is working.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Objective | **Engagement** |
| Campaign name | `EVZO · Δωρεάν διατροφολόγιο · Oct` |
| Buying type | Auction |
| Advantage campaign budget | **On** |
| Daily budget | €20 to start. Do not touch it for 4 days |
| Special ad category | **None** — see the warning below |

### The special-ad-category question

Meta flags weight loss under some health and wellbeing rules, and declaring a
special category costs you age and detailed targeting. EVZO sells meal planning
information, not a medical or health treatment, so **None** is correct. If the
campaign is rejected on that basis, do not argue it in an appeal: change the
creative that triggered it. Before-and-after images, body-shaming language and
promises of a specific weight by a specific date are what get these ads pulled.
None of the five frames contain any of that, which is deliberate.

### Audience

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Locations | **Cyprus** and **Greece** |
| Age | **28 – 55** |
| Gender | All |
| Advantage+ audience | **On** |
| Detailed targeting | **Leave empty** |
| Languages | **Greek** |

Leave the interests empty on purpose. Meta's delivery finds this audience from
who engages far faster than an interest list does, and "διατροφή" or "fitness"
interests mostly reach people who already follow twenty coaches. Set the
language to Greek or the ads go to English-speaking expats who will not read a
word of them.

### Placements

**Advantage+ placements, on.** That is what the two ratios are for: the 1:1 goes
to feed and the 9:16 to Stories and Reels, from the same ad.

## 4. The greeting in the DM

Every ad opens a chat. Set this as the message template so nobody lands in an
empty thread:

```
Γεια σου! Στείλε μου «ΠΛΑΝΟ» και σου στέλνω δωρεάν διατροφολόγιο στα μέτρα σου.

Θα χρειαστώ 4 πράγματα:
1. Στόχο: χάσιμο, συντήρηση ή όγκος
2. Ύψος και βάρος
3. Ηλικία
4. Κάτι που δεν τρως ή δεν αντέχεις
```

Four questions, not ten. Every extra one loses people, and the rest can be
asked once they are already talking.

---

## 5. After it is live

Leave it alone for four days. Meta needs that long before its numbers mean
anything, and changing the budget restarts the learning phase.

**Then read it in this order:**

1. **Cost per conversation.** Under €3 is working, over €8 is not.
2. **Which ad won.** Turn off anything at double the winner's cost.
3. **How many conversations became sales.** This is the one that matters, and
   nothing in Ads Manager can tell you. Count it by hand.

**The number that decides everything:** at €20 a day you will get somewhere
between five and fifteen conversations a day. Every one is a plan you write
yourself. Three days in, ask whether you can keep up. If you cannot, that is
the generator being needed, not the ads failing.
