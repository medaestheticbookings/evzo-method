# EVZO — logo pack

## The idea
The mark is the wordmark, stacked. **EV** over **ZO** — two lines, equal width, no illustration.
It reads at 32 px, it embroiders, and it can't be confused with the hundred fitness pages
using a dumbbell silhouette.

The secondary mark is three descending bars. It is the bar chart from the Protein Score
posts and it reads as a number coming down. Use it as a favicon, a watermark, or a
repeat pattern on merch — never as a replacement for the wordmark on the profile.

## Colours
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Navy (ground) | `#0B1220` |
| Yellow (accent) | `#FFE14D` |
| Ink (on yellow) | `#06101B` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |

## What to use where
- **Instagram profile picture** → `evzo-profile-navy.png` (1080×1080)
- **Website / email header** → `evzo-lockup-dark.png` or the SVG
- **On a photo or a busy background** → `evzo-profile-mono.png`
- **Merch, one-colour print, embroidery** → `evzo-wordmark-black.svg` or `evzo-wordmark-white.svg`
- **Anywhere the header on your slides appears** → `evzo-wordmark-twotone` (EV white, ZO yellow)

## Rules
1. Keep clear space of one "E" width around the wordmark on all sides.
2. Never stretch, outline, add a drop shadow, or rotate it.
3. On yellow, the letters go ink `#06101B` — never white.
4. Below about 24 px, use the stacked square, not the horizontal lockup.

## Files
Every logo is supplied as **SVG with the letters converted to outlines**, so it prints and
embroiders anywhere without needing the font installed. PNGs are exported at 1080 px
(square) and 1600–1800 px (wide), transparent where it makes sense.

## Fonts
Wordmark: **Archivo Black**. Slide headings: **Bebas Neue**. Body: **Inter**. Labels: **Barlow Condensed**.
