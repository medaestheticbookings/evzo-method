    Why you can trust the figures

    Every number here is computed, not claimed

    Published equations, named

    Your maintenance calories come from the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, and your protein target from body weight. Both are standard and both are stated on your guide, so you can check them.

    Calories come from the ingredient

    Every figure on every recipe is added up from the ingredient list rather than typed in by hand. That is why the numbers on this page match the numbers in the books.

    Ranges, not false precision

    You get a range, not a single number pretending to be exact. Anyone quoting you 1,847 calories to the calorie is selling certainty that does not exist.

    What this is not

    Not medical or dietetic advice, and we do not pretend otherwise. It is educational, it is general, and where it is not right for you the page says so instead of taking your money.

      Questions

      Before you ask

      Is this suitable for me?

      It is built for healthy adults over 18 who want structure around ordinary food. It is not suitable if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a diagnosed medical condition, a history of disordered eating, or follow a medically prescribed diet. The questionnaire asks about all of those before anything is built, and will stop rather than sell you something wrong.

      How long do the meals take to cook?

      You tell us before the plan is built — fifteen minutes, half an hour, an hour — and nothing that takes longer goes in it. Most of what we build lands between fifteen and thirty minutes, because a plan you have no time to cook is not a plan.

      Do I need special ingredients or supplements?

      No. Everything comes from an ordinary supermarket in Cyprus or Greece — chicken, yoghurt, lentils, rice, olive oil, whatever is in season. No powders, no imported health foods, nothing you have to order.

      What if I do not like what is in it?

      You tell us what you will not eat before it is built, and those foods never appear. Every meal also comes with an alternative, so a night you cannot face the plan does not end the week.
